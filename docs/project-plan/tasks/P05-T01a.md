# P05-T01a — Pure approved reserve-solvency policy

**Status:** Complete as pure policy (2026-10-19); [CI 39209862800](https://github.com/vishweshgawas06-dotcom/fairmount-games/actions/runs/39209862800) passed. Live betting/ledger gates remain open.

The owner approved [POOL-01](../decisions/2026-10-19-funded-reserve-solvency.md): after transferring a candidate accepted stake into the separately funded reserve, its balance must cover the sum across unsettled rounds of each round's larger obligation—its highest *total* payout for numbers 0–36, or a full void refund of all accepted stakes. Reject a new candidate **in full** for insufficient reserve, not an already accepted bet; never choose an outcome from liability. This rule does not weaken the separately approved cap-limited partial-acceptance behavior.

`fairmount_api.reserve_coverage` is a pure integer-only helper validating one immutable 37-outcome exposure vector plus full-refund total per UUID-identified unsettled round. It computes each round's maximum obligation, sums independent-round maxima and compares `balance + candidate_accepted_stake` with the resulting obligation. Invalid shapes, bools, floats, negatives, zero candidate stake, empty resulting rounds and refund totals smaller than the candidate stake fail closed. The refund-total check is only a sanity guard: it cannot prove the candidate was actually included; the future locked bet/ledger transaction must establish that. It does not accept bets, compute payouts from positions, update a wallet, persist projections or enforce locking. Test examples include a 10-point straight-up bet paying 350 **total**, void refund dominating payouts, and different unresolved rounds with different worst-case numbers.

**Boundary:** Caller-supplied exposures must come exclusively from authoritative accepted bets/candidate after the separately approved position/cap and wallet checks, never the browser. The future PostgreSQL acceptance operation must atomically transfer the stake, recalculate/lock all exposure and reserve rows, enforce cutoff/idempotency and test races, conservation, settlement/refund and withdrawals. D-06 and operational failure handling remain unresolved; no live global bet restriction exists yet.
