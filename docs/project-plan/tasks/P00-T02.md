# P00-T02 — Approve roulette bet types and payout rules

**Status:** Open  
**Phase:** P00 — Specification lock  
**Priority:** Blocking for betting-engine implementation

## Objective

Approve the V1 bet catalogue and precise payout/settlement conventions.

## Scope

Document each supported bet, selection format, stake validation, payout, zero handling, and interaction with the wallet ledger.

## Questions requiring approval

1. Which inside bets are in scope?
2. Which outside bets are in scope?
3. Are payout figures net winnings or total return including the original stake?
4. How does zero affect each supported outside bet?
5. Are duplicate bets on the same selection allowed?
6. Can a player remove/edit a bet before betting closes?
7. What happens when the available balance changes or becomes insufficient before acceptance?
8. How are voided rounds and settlement retries handled?

## Acceptance criteria

- Complete bet catalogue with valid selections.
- Exact payout and zero-handling rules.
- Explicit stake validation and edit/cancel policy.
- Transactional/idempotent settlement behavior specified.
- Owner approval recorded before implementation.
- Related requirements and traceability updated.

## Out of scope

Do not implement the betting engine in this task. Do not alter outcomes based on bets or player balances.
