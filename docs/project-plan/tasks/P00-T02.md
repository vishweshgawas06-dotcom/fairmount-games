## Objective

Approve the V1 bet catalogue and precise payout/settlement conventions before implementing the betting engine.

## Current draft

See [Roulette Payout and Settlement Specification (Draft)](../roulette/PAYOUT_AND_SETTLEMENT_SPEC_DRAFT.md).

## Owner rules recorded (not full-spec approval)

- Payout multipliers calculate total payout including the stake; e.g. a 10-coin straight-up win credits 350 coins total.
- One server-authoritative global outcome is shared by all players; each player sees only their own bet data.
- Chips are 5, 10, 50, 100, and 500 coins; minimum stake is 5.
- Per-number exposure cap: 2,000 coins across straight-up and split bets covering that number. A split contributes its stake to both numbers.
- A 2,000-coin combined per-player/per-round cap is intended for each exact Red, Black, dozen, column, Odd, Even, and row/street selection. Duplicate records count toward the combined cap.
- Duplicate identical selections remain separate records, with combined stake shown in the UI.
- A bet may be edited/cancelled only before server acceptance; accepted bets are immutable. The draft proposes accepting only the valid amount remaining under a cap and immediately returning excess.
- Rebet repeats only that player's previous-round set, all-or-nothing; accepted rebet bets are immutable.
- Timing: seconds 0–44 betting open; second 45 final-bet call; second 50 close/freeze accepted bets and start animation; second 60 reveal result and settle.
- Player-side disconnection after acceptance does not cancel a bet.
- Proposed server/game-failure recovery: if the round cannot safely complete before settlement commits, void it and refund all accepted stakes idempotently. Use one atomic round-settlement transaction; never regenerate a persisted outcome; do not refund after a successful settlement commit.
- Standard European single-zero layout only, with valid standard selections.

## Remaining questions before full approval

1. Confirm whether the 2,000-coin cap also applies per player, per round, per selection to corner, six-line, first-four, and Low/High.
2. Confirm that “rows” means street/row bets.
3. Review the exact canonical position catalogue, especially zero-adjacent bets.
4. Confirm the proposed local-draft interaction: edit/cancel before submission; after server acceptance, the bet is immutable.
5. Confirm the proposed server/game-failure policy: atomic settlement; if the round fails before settlement commits, void/refund all accepted stakes; if settlement commits successfully, do not refund; never regenerate a persisted outcome.
6. Explicit owner approval of the complete specification.

## Acceptance criteria

- Complete catalogue and exact payout multipliers.
- Explicit zero handling, chip rules, exposure caps, duplicate behavior, partial acceptance, and bet finality.
- Player-private bet visibility enforced at API/realtime layer.
- Rebet is player-specific, all-or-nothing, and subject to balance, limits, and cutoff.
- Round timing, wallet credit, disconnect behavior, and server-failure recovery are unambiguous.
- Transactional/idempotent settlement and refunds are specified.
- Full owner approval and acceptance evidence are recorded before implementation.

Do not implement the betting engine in this task. Do not alter outcomes based on bets or balances. Do not mark P00-T02 complete until all open questions are resolved and the owner explicitly approves the complete specification.
