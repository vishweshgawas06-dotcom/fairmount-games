## Owner rules recorded (not full-spec approval)

- Payout multipliers calculate total payout including the stake; e.g. a 10-coin straight-up win credits 350 coins total.
- One server-authoritative global outcome is shared by all players; each player sees only their own bet data.
- Chips are 5, 10, 50, 100, and 500 coins; minimum stake is 5.
- Per-number exposure cap: 2,000 coins across straight-up and split bets covering that number. A split contributes its stake to both numbers.
- A 2,000-coin combined per-player/per-round cap is intended for each exact Red, Black, dozen, column, Odd, Even, and row/street selection. Corner, six-line, first-four, and Low/High cap treatment remains to be confirmed.
- Duplicate identical selections remain separate records, with combined stake shown in the UI.
- A bet can be edited/cancelled only before server acceptance. Once accepted, it is immutable. If a request exceeds a cap, the draft proposes accepting the valid remainder and returning excess immediately, subject to chip rules.
- Rebet repeats only that player's prior-round set, all-or-nothing; accepted rebet bets are immutable.
- Timing: seconds 0–44 betting open; second 45 final-bet call; second 50 close/freeze accepted bets and start animation; second 60 reveal result and settle.
- Player-side disconnect after acceptance does not cancel a bet. Server/game-side failure that prevents safe completion should automatically refund affected accepted stakes with auditable, idempotent accounting.
- Standard European single-zero layout only, with valid straight-up, split, street, corner, six-line, first-four, and supported outside bets.

## Remaining questions before full approval

1. Does the 2,000-coin cap also apply per selection to corner, six-line, first-four, and Low/High?
2. Does “rows” mean street/row bets?
3. Confirm partial acceptance to the remaining cap and immediate return of excess, including for split bets.
4. Confirm the proposed pre-acceptance UI: edit/cancel a local draft before submission; after server acceptance, the bet is immutable.
5. Review the exact canonical position catalogue, especially zero-adjacent bets.
6. Confirm the recommended server-failure recovery: if no outcome is persisted, void/refund; if persisted, reuse it if safe, otherwise void/refund; never regenerate a persisted outcome, and never both pay and refund the same stake.
7. Explicit owner approval of the complete specification.

## Acceptance criteria

- Complete catalogue and exact payout multipliers.
- Explicit zero handling, chip rules, exposure caps, duplicate behavior, partial acceptance, and bet finality.
- Player-private bet visibility enforced at API/realtime layer.
- Rebet is player-specific, all-or-nothing, and subject to balance, limits, and cutoff.
- Round timing, wallet credit, disconnect behavior, and server-failure recovery are unambiguous.
- Transactional/idempotent settlement and refunds are specified.
- Full owner approval and acceptance evidence are recorded before implementation.

## Out of scope

Do not implement the betting engine in this task. Do not alter outcomes based on bets or balances. Do not mark P00-T02 complete until all open questions are resolved and the owner explicitly approves the complete specification.
