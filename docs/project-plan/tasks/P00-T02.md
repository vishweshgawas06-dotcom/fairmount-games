## Owner-approved rules

- Payout multipliers calculate total payout including the stake; e.g. a 10-coin straight-up win credits 350 coins total.
- One server-authoritative global outcome is shared by all players; each player sees only their own bet data.
- Chips are 5, 10, 50, 100, and 500 coins; minimum stake is 5.
- Per-number exposure cap: 2,000 coins across straight-up and split bets covering that number. A split contributes its stake to both numbers.
- A 2,000-coin combined per-player/per-round cap applies to each exact Red, Black, dozen, column, street/row, Odd, Even, corner, six-line, first-four, and Low/High selection. Duplicate records count toward the same exact-selection cap.
- Duplicate identical selections remain separate records, with combined stake shown in the UI.
- Players may edit/cancel a local bet draft before submission; accepted bets are immutable. If the player's balance is less than an ordinary requested stake, reject the entire bet without changing the wallet. If balance covers the full requested stake but a cap limits acceptance, accept only the valid remaining amount when chip/minimum rules allow and immediately return the cap-excess.
- Rebet repeats only that player's previous-round set, all-or-nothing; accepted rebet bets are immutable.
- Timing: seconds 0–44 betting open; second 45 final-bet call; second 50 close/freeze accepted bets and start animation; second 60 reveal result and settle.
- Player-side disconnection after acceptance does not cancel a bet.
- [D-06a](../decisions/2026-10-24-round-result-recovery.md) supersedes the 2026-10-14 automatic failure-void choice: retry an independent missing result after cutoff and settle even late; reuse a persisted result and retry settlement, never reroll or auto-void. Exceptional incident void requires a separately authorized policy; do not refund a persisted-result bet.
- Standard European single-zero layout only, with valid standard selections.

## Owner choices confirmed on 2026-10-14

- The 2,000-coin combined cap applies to each exact corner, six-line, first-four, and Low/High selection too.
- “Rows” means street bets.
- Editing/cancellation happens in a local draft before submission; accepted bets are immutable.
- **Historical 2026-10-14 choice, superseded by D-06a on 2026-10-24:** automatic void/refund when settlement cannot commit is no longer approved. Settlement/retries remain atomic and idempotent; exceptional incident void is a separate unresolved authorization decision.
- Insufficient-balance policy is locked: reject an ordinary bet in full if the wallet cannot cover the requested stake. Cap-limited partial acceptance is allowed only when the wallet covers the full requested stake.
