# P00-T02 — Approve roulette bet types and payout rules

**Status:** Open — owner choices recorded; full specification approval pending  
**Phase:** P00 — Specification lock  
**Priority:** Blocking for betting-engine implementation

## Objective

Approve the V1 bet catalogue and precise payout/settlement conventions before implementing the betting engine.

## Current draft

See [Roulette Payout and Settlement Specification (Draft)](../roulette/PAYOUT_AND_SETTLEMENT_SPEC_DRAFT.md).

## Owner choices recorded (not full-spec approval)

1. Use the original conventional payout ratios as multipliers for the **total payout credited**, including the placed stake. Example: a 10-coin straight-up win credits 350 coins total, not 360. Do not return the stake a second time.
2. Each player sees their own individual roulette table/interface and own bets only. Other players' bet amounts and selections must not be exposed.
3. Maximum stake is 2,000 coins per individual bet selection, not a total-round cap. It applies to all bet types; the earlier proposed 3,000-coin cap for other categories is superseded.
4. Rebet repeats that player's own previous bet set and amounts. If the balance cannot cover the entire set, place none. If accepted, the player can edit/add/remove bets before betting closes.
5. At second 45, show the final-bet call. At second 50, close and freeze bets and start the wheel/ball animation. At second 60, reveal the result, aggregate each player's winning payouts, and credit the wallet.
6. All players share one server-authoritative round and result, while bet information remains private to each player. Outcome generation must be independent of bets and balances; settlement must be transactional and idempotent.
7. Minimum stake is 5 coins; allowed chip denominations are 5, 10, 50, 100, and 500.
8. Duplicate identical selections remain separate bet records, with their combined stake shown clearly in the interface.
9. Bet edits are atomic: validate first; accept the complete requested change or leave the existing accepted state unchanged.
10. If a round cannot safely complete, freeze settlement for owner/admin review.
11. Use the standard European roulette layout and allow only valid standard selections.

## Remaining questions requiring resolution before full approval

- Does the 2,000-coin cap apply to the combined stake across duplicate records for the same exact selection, or separately to each record?
- What is the atomic scope for an edit that affects multiple selections: one submitted operation as a whole, or one selection at a time?
- What explicit decisions can owner/admin take for a frozen/void round (refund, resume using persisted outcome, other), and what if no decision is made promptly?
- Publish the canonical valid-position mapping for all standard inside bets, especially zero-adjacent splits/streets, corners/six lines near zero, and first four.
- Define failure handling depending on whether an outcome has not yet been generated, has been persisted, or has been displayed. A persisted outcome must never be regenerated to alter a round.
- Owner approval of the complete specification is required before implementation.

## Acceptance criteria

- Complete bet catalogue with valid selections and exact total-payout multipliers.
- Explicit zero-handling, chip/stake validation, duplicate-selection behavior, and stake-limit rules.
- Player-private bet visibility enforced at API/realtime layer, not only by hiding UI elements.
- Rebet is player-specific, all-or-nothing, and subject to balance, limits, and cutoff.
- Exact round timing and wallet-credit timing are documented.
- Void-round and failure handling is unambiguous.
- Transactional/idempotent settlement behavior is specified.
- Owner approval is recorded before implementation.
- Related requirements and traceability documents are updated.

## Out of scope

Do not implement the betting engine in this task. Do not alter outcomes based on bets or player balances. Do not mark this task complete until the owner approves the full specification and acceptance evidence is recorded.
