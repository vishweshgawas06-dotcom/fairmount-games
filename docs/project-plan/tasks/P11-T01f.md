# P11-T01f — Concurrent admission versus full-round void

**Status:** [PostgreSQL CI 39916710875](https://github.com/vishweshgawas06-dotcom/fairmount-games/actions/runs/39916710875) passed (2026-10-23). D-06 remains unresolved, and no production recovery decision is made by this test-only call.

Extend the disposable migrated PostgreSQL contention fixture: after the previous round's refund restores the reserve, open a fresh round. Simultaneously submit one funded straight-up bet and a trusted full-round void from **different committed connections**. The accepted bet may commit first and be refunded, or the void may commit first and the bet must be rejected. Neither interleaving may leave an unpaid accepted bet, partial debit, second payout or continuing unsettled liability. Assert immutable bet/refund counts match, reserve and player wallets equal their pre-race amounts, and the supply/journal reconcile under a stable snapshot.

This covers concurrent admission/void serialization only. It does not authorize arbitrary recovery, prove all interleavings, or replace payout/void conflict and winner-settlement concurrency tests.
