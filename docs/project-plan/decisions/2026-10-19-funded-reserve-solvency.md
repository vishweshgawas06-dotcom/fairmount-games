# POOL-01 — Funded reserve admission and void coverage

**Status:** Owner-approved product rule on 2026-10-19. Pure policy/fixture tests may proceed; live bet acceptance still requires atomic wallet/round/exposure implementation, incident handling and PostgreSQL concurrency evidence.

## Context and alternatives

D-10 selected a separate owner-funded game reserve instead of outcomes shaped by liability or silent payout issuance. The prospective global acceptance restriction was explicitly left open because it can prevent otherwise legal, affordable bets. The owner was asked to approve an admission rule: the reserve, after adding the candidate's accepted stake, must cover the maximum total payout of all unsettled accepted bets for every possible winning number; if not, reject the **new** bet. The owner approved. A further question established that an entire round may be void-refunded; refunding all accepted stakes can be a larger obligation than any number's payout. The owner approved including full void refunds as an alternative obligation.

Alternatives not chosen: accept underfunded bets and hope the owner funds later; pay only some winners; manipulate the winning number; silently create points; use reserve insufficiency to cancel/partially pay already accepted bets; count only winning payouts but not void refunds.

## Approved rule

- The owner may fund/replenish the separate reserve only by an audited transfer of existing owner-wallet points. Owner-only, reasoned issuance first credits the owner wallet as a *distinct* operation; gameplay does not issue points. Accepted stakes move into the reserve in linked ledger effects. A payout or void refund debits that reserve and credits the player exactly once, under the approved total-return multipliers.
- For each unsettled round `r`, let `P(r,n)` be the **sum of total return** owed for that round's accepted bets if the independent result is `n`, for every `n ∈ {0,…,36}`. Let `V(r)` be the sum of **all** accepted stakes that would be returned if that entire round is voided. Its funded obligation is `O(r) = max(V(r), max_n P(r,n))`. Across unsettled rounds, the safe combined liability is `O = Σ_r O(r)`; distinct unresolved rounds may have different winning numbers or independently be voided. Settled/refunded terminal rounds have zero remaining obligation.
- Before accepting a candidate bet with cap-approved accepted stake `S`, compute the resulting payout/refund exposures **including** that candidate. Admit it only if `current_reserve_balance + S ≥ resulting O`, then atomically move `S` from the player to the reserve and persist the accepted bet/exposure. Otherwise reject the candidate **entirely for reserve insufficiency**, leaving wallet, stake and accepted bets unchanged. This is prospective only: it never rewrites, cancels or withholds an accepted bet. It does not alter the approved cap-limited partial-acceptance rule or introduce reserve-limited partial stakes. A wallet shortfall still rejects the entire requested bet before any cap partial acceptance.
- A reserve withdrawal, if later offered, must preserve `remaining_reserve ≥ O` in the same locked transaction; the owner cannot drain funded obligations. Reserve replenishment is a separate owner-authorized audited transfer, not an automatic requirement imposed on the user. On insufficient admission, show a clear non-sensitive rejection and allow later retry after explicit funding, subject to cutoff. Do not regenerate outcomes, favor low-liability numbers or default on accepted payouts/refunds.

## Engineering gate (not an implemented endpoint)

The future acceptance transaction must lock the global round/cutoff, reserve balance, player wallet, exposure projections and idempotency rows in a reviewed stable order; derive all 37 outcome totals from the **approved legal-position catalogue and payout multipliers**; preserve global invariants across simultaneous players/rounds; and reconcile wallet/ledger/bet/obligation projections. Pure policy tests are necessary but do not establish concurrent safety. If an unexpected post-acceptance shortfall occurs, stop accepting new bets, protect accepted obligations, alert the owner and use explicit audited replenishment/repair; do not invent a payout, refund or round-void timeout. D-06 round recovery and operational key/backup/SLO planning remain open.

D-02 retention/deletion/backups and manual all-factors-lost owner recovery remain unresolved. This decision does not authorize destructive cleanup, self-service recovery, live betting before its prerequisites or real-money wagering.
