## Substantive incompatibilities; do not resolve by choosing one side wholesale

1. **Identity/storage:** This branch uses UUID accounts, separate private identities, encrypted owner MFA, digest-only role-specific sessions, append-only security events and a sequence of Alembic revisions. `main`'s engine models use BIGINT account IDs with name/mobile directly on the account, a different `Base`, a single incompatible `0001_initial_schema` migration and no corresponding credential/MFA/session tables. Roulette FKs and tests are keyed to those BIGINT IDs. Keeping either `db/models.py` wholesale would discard the other schema and break code/tests; a blind merge of migration trees would not be a valid single schema history.
2. **Point accounting:** `main`'s `wallet.service.admin_adjust` directly credits/debits an arbitrary wallet given `actor_id`; that internal function is not owner-only audited issuance into the owner wallet or paired funded owner↔agent/player transfers. The roulette engine also lacks the separately funded reserve and the later owner-approved [POOL-01](decisions/2026-10-19-funded-reserve-solvency.md) worst-payout/full-refund admission invariant. Do **not** expose these functions as authenticated money-moving routes or assume independent engine tests establish approved V1 accounting.
3. **Round recovery:** `main`'s `recover_round` voids after a configurable `max_attempts` for any exception. D-06 explicitly leaves the definition of irrecoverable failure, retry deadline and no-outcome recovery open; the approved rules forbid an arbitrary retry cutoff or outcome regeneration. Treat this service as unapproved for live round recovery even though it has tests.
4. **Positive reusable work:** The independent RNG path calls `secrets.randbelow(37)` without bet/stake/balance inputs, and the engine includes catalogue/payout/cap/domain tests. Port and retest these against the approved fixture and this branch's secure identity and future funded-ledger model. Its independent CI success is evidence for its *own* checkout, not for integrated behavior.
